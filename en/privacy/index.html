Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 15 October 2026 (Catbeam 1.1.0) · This English version applies in every country except Türkiye, where the Turkish version applies. Translations into Japanese, Korean and Chinese are provided for convenience.

In short: no account, no tracking. Your cat records, health journal and any audio you save stay on your iPhone (and, if you switch on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud, which we cannot access). Data leaves your iPhone only when you create your living cat or buy a special-day clip or the Special Days, All Year pack: the 1–4 cat photos (for a special-day clip and the yearly pack, your cat's picture in its room instead of photos), the cat's name, the details you choose to enter about the cat (such as eye colour, age or breed), Apple's signed proof of your purchase and, only when a living cat is created and you allow notifications, a push token. Photos are processed by our server and by two providers in the USA (Anthropic and Higgsfield). Our server deletes the job record at the latest 7 days after your iPhone has received your cat, and never keeps it more than 30 days after the cat is ready.

1. Who is responsible

Catbeam is published by Batuhan Eren Engin, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the controller of the personal data described here (the seller shown on the App Store). Contact: erengn@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Microphone and audio (cat translator): the cat translator analyses sound on your iPhone with Apple's built-in sound classifier and the app's own calculation. Audio is never sent to any server: not to us, not to Apple, not to any other company. Short recordings you choose to keep stay on your iPhone only; they are not part of iCloud sync (they may be part of your iPhone backup).
  • Cat profiles, health records, vaccinations, medication, weight, memories, reminders, translation history: stored in the app's database on your iPhone. Not sent to us.
  • Smart care guide (health and behaviour questions): answered by Apple's on-device model or the app's built-in guide. In this version the guide connects to no server (not ours, not an AI provider's); we do not receive your questions and they do not leave your iPhone.
  • Reminders: local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Special-day notifications: notifications for your cat's birthday, home day and days such as Christmas, New Year, Halloween, Cat Day or Lunar New Year are scheduled locally on your iPhone (the notification title carries your cat's name). These dates and notifications are not sent to us; you can switch them off in Settings → "Special-day notifications". The yearly pack does not change that: the dates stay on your iPhone (see section 3).
  • Push token (APNs): if you allow notifications, iOS gives the app a device token for Apple's push service. It is sent to our server only while a living cat is being created, for the "your cat is ready" notification (section 3); it is not sent for special-day clips, the yearly pack or reminders.
  • Home Screen widget and app icon: the widget shows a small snapshot (your cat's name, a still picture and one care note) that the app saves in a storage area shared only between Catbeam and its widget on your iPhone (an App Group). It is never sent to us. The app icon you choose is kept by iOS.
  • iCloud sync (optional): if iCloud is available and switched on for Catbeam, the following are stored in your own iCloud private database (Apple's CloudKit, under your Apple Account; Apple runs this storage under its own terms): cat profiles (name, profile photo, everything you entered, birthday and home day), health records, vaccine and medication records, weight, memories (notes and the photos you add), reminders, translation history (not the audio files), special-day clip records, the Special Days, All Year pack's entitlement details (entitlement number, entitlement secret, cat code and Apple's signed transaction record), and your cat's pack files and special-day clips (videos and pictures). This way they reach your other devices and a new iPhone. We do not have access to this storage and never receive this data; it does not pass through our server. You can switch sync off in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Catbeam; while it is off, everything stays on your iPhone only. "Delete all my data" also deletes the files and records this iPhone put into iCloud (see section 7).
  • Share videos: the "teleport" video you can share is made on your iPhone and goes only to the app you pick in the share sheet.
  • Background refresh: iOS may wake the app briefly to top up local medication reminders while a treatment course is running; nothing leaves your iPhone for that. While a living cat is being created, a background check (and the silent "ready" notification) asks our server for the status of that job, using the job's number; see section 3.

3. Data that leaves your iPhone: creating your living cat

When you choose "Bring your cat to life", the app first shows a consent screen explaining what is sent and to whom. Only after you agree does it send the following over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Photos are re-encoded on your iPhone before upload, which removes location and camera metadata. If you pick a video, the video itself never leaves your iPhone: it is analysed on the device, and only up to 4 still frames chosen from it are re-encoded like photos (without location or camera metadata) and sent.

Special Days, All Year pack (per cat, 12 months). If you buy this pack for a cat, the seven special-day clips of that cat for 12 months from the purchase (birthday, home day, Christmas, New Year, Lunar New Year, Halloween and Cat Day) are prepared when the day comes; no separate purchase is needed for each clip. Our server keeps none of your cat's dates and no pictures for the pack. The birthday and home-day dates and the pictures stay on your iPhone (and, if you switched on iCloud sync, in your own iCloud like the rest of your cat's details). The record our server keeps for the entitlement is only this: an entitlement number; a one-way hash of Apple's transaction ID; a one-way hash of a random, anonymous code of your cat (not its name; it cannot be turned back into it); a random entitlement secret your iPhone presents when it uses the entitlement; which of the seven days have been used, and when; the date the pack is valid until; the App Store country code of the purchase and the quality tier (A or B) chosen from it; and the total generation cost spent on the entitlement (an anonymous counter). When a day comes and your iPhone prepares the clip (this needs the app to run: it tries early on the day in the background, otherwise when you open it; it can start up to 7 days after the day itself), it sends the same things as for a single special-day clip described above (your cat's room picture, its name and its looks), together with that day's date and the entitlement secret, to the same three recipients for the same purpose; they are used for that one clip and are not kept for later days. The clip itself is kept and deleted as described above. Retention and deletion: the entitlement record is deleted automatically 30 days after the date the pack is valid until. If you want it gone sooner, Settings → Data → "Delete all my data" deletes it, and the clip jobs tied to it, from our server at once (the request may not reach the server while your iPhone is offline; if so, email us, section 10). A deleted entitlement cannot be activated again with the same purchase; to prevent that, only a one-way hash of Apple's transaction ID is kept, until 30 days after the date the pack was valid until. Notifications are still scheduled locally on your iPhone.

We ask for your explicit consent for these transfers on the in-app consent screen or on the short consent page shown before you buy a special-day clip; if you do not consent nothing is sent and the rest of the app keeps working.

4. Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details, name or Apple Account. The app reads your purchases from Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone. When you create a living cat, for a special-day clip you bought, and when you activate the Special Days, All Year pack, the Apple-signed transaction information (transaction ID, product, environment and the App Store country of the purchase) is sent to our server (with the photos for creation, with the relevant picture for a special-day clip) so that we can check the purchase is valid and limit generation per purchase (one cat plus two retries; one clip per special-day purchase; seven special days, each once, over 12 months for the yearly pack). If a purchase is refunded, our server does not create new cats or clips for it. Catbeam Plus is checked only on your iPhone; nothing about it is sent to us.

5. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that.

6. What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, advertising identifier or usage analytics; the app contains no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK, and you do not create an account. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not sell personal information; it shares it with nobody except the service providers described above.

7. Deleting your data

  • On your iPhone: Settings → Data → "Delete all my data" removes cat profiles, health records, memories, reminders, translation history, saved recordings, generated cats, special-day clips and their records, any photos kept until your cat's moves are installed, and your upload consent. If iCloud sync is on, the pack and clip files this iPhone put into iCloud are deleted from iCloud too, the deletion of the records syncs to your other devices, and the server record of a Special Days, All Year entitlement is deleted (section 3). Deleting the app removes everything on the device; for the copies in iCloud, use "Delete all my data" first or delete Catbeam's data in your iPhone's iCloud storage settings.
  • On our server: the job record, together with the files of your cat's pack on our server (videos and room pictures), is deleted automatically within 7 days after the app on your iPhone confirms it has received your cat; those days let the remaining moves still download and let us hand over a lost download again. If that confirmation never arrives, it is deleted 30 days after your cat is ready. A job that produces no cat is deleted within 24 hours of its start (at most 7 days if it is held for review). With no account we hold nothing that identifies you; if you want it deleted sooner, email us with the approximate time of the upload.
  • Special-day clips, the yearly pack and other records: a special-day clip job is deleted 3 days after the clip reaches your iPhone, 30 days after it is ready if it never does, and 24 hours after a failed attempt. A yearly pack's entitlement record is deleted 30 days after its valid-until date (or at once with "Delete all my data"). Purchase counters are deleted 12 months after last use and server logs after 30 days.

9. International transfers

The controller is in Türkiye, our server is in Germany (EU), and Anthropic and Higgsfield (with its partner models, such as those of Alibaba, Kuaishou and MiniMax) process data in the United States and possibly other countries. Data is transferred abroad only to provide the service you ask for. Before any upload, the in-app consent screen names the recipients and that they are in the USA; the transfer is based on the explicit consent you give there (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR / UK GDPR). These countries may not offer the same level of data protection as your own. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → "Withdraw consent"; this stops future uploads.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, data portability, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). Email erengn@gmail.com; because there is no account, mention the approximate time of your upload so we can find the job. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the authority where you live or work in the EEA, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or the Swiss FDPIC.

Data protection officer: we have not appointed one, because our processing does not meet the conditions that require it (Art. 37 GDPR). Contact us at the address above. EU/UK representative: none has been appointed. Requests from the EU, the UK and Türkiye (KVKK) are handled at erengn@gmail.com.
