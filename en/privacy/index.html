Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · This English version applies in every country except Türkiye, where the Turkish version applies. Translations into Japanese, Korean and Chinese are provided for convenience.

In short: no account, no ads, no tracking. Your cat records, health journal and audio stay on your iPhone. Data leaves your iPhone only when you create your living cat: the 1–4 cat photos, the cat's name, the details you choose to enter about the cat (such as eye colour, age or breed), Apple's signed proof of your purchase and, if you allow notifications, a push token. Photos are processed by our server and by two providers in the USA (Anthropic and Higgsfield). Our server deletes the job record at the latest 7 days after your iPhone has received your cat, and never keeps it more than 30 days after the cat is ready.

1. Who is responsible

Catbeam is published by Batuhan Eren Engin, an individual developer based in Türkiye, who is the controller of the personal data described here (the seller shown on the App Store). Contact: erengn@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Microphone and audio: the cat translator analyses sound on your iPhone with Apple's built-in sound classifier. Audio is never uploaded.
  • Cat profiles, health records, vaccinations, medication, weight, memories, reminders, translation history: stored in the app's database on your iPhone. Not sent to us.
  • Health and behaviour questions: answered by Apple's on-device model or the app's built-in guide. We do not receive your questions.
  • Reminders: local notifications scheduled on your iPhone.

3. Data that leaves your iPhone: creating your living cat

When you choose "Bring your cat to life", the app first shows a consent screen explaining what is sent and to whom. Only after you agree does it send the following over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Photos are re-encoded on your iPhone before upload, which removes location and camera metadata.

If you do not agree, nothing is uploaded and no living cat is created; you can keep using the rest of the app and the ready-made cats. You can withdraw consent in Settings → Privacy → "Withdraw consent"; this stops future uploads.

4. Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your card details, name or Apple Account. The app reads your purchases from Apple's StoreKit on your iPhone. When you create a living cat, the Apple-signed transaction information (transaction ID, product, environment) is sent to our server with the photos so that we can check the purchase is valid and limit generation per purchase (one cat plus two retries). If a purchase is refunded, our server does not create new cats for it. Catbeam Plus is checked only on your iPhone; nothing about it is sent to us.

5. When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write. We use it only to answer you and keep it only as long as needed for that.

6. What we do not collect

No account, name, phone number, location, contacts, advertising identifier, usage analytics, crash-reporting SDK or advertising SDK. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites and does not sell or share personal information.

7. Deleting your data

  • On your iPhone: Settings → Data → "Delete all my data" removes cat profiles, health records, memories, reminders, translation history, generated cats, any photos kept until your cat's moves are installed, and your upload consent. Deleting the app also removes everything on the device.
  • On our server: the job record, together with any copy of your generated images and videos, is deleted automatically within 7 days after the app on your iPhone confirms it has received your cat; those days let the remaining moves still download and let us hand over a lost download again. If that confirmation never arrives, it is deleted 30 days after your cat is ready. A job that produces no cat is deleted within 24 hours of its start (at most 7 days if it is held for review). With no account we hold nothing that identifies you; if you want it deleted sooner, email us with the approximate time of the upload.

9. International transfers

The controller is in Türkiye, our server is in Germany (EU), and Anthropic and Higgsfield (with its partner models) process data in the United States and possibly other countries. Data is transferred abroad only to provide the service you ask for. Before any upload, the in-app consent screen names the recipients and that they are in the USA; the transfer is based on the explicit consent you give there (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR / UK GDPR). These countries may not offer the same level of data protection as your own. You can withdraw consent at any time in Settings → Privacy → "Withdraw consent"; this stops future uploads.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, restriction of processing, data portability, object to processing based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time (without affecting earlier processing). Email erengn@gmail.com; because there is no account, mention the approximate time of your upload so we can find the job. We answer within one month.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the authority where you live or work in the EEA, the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or the Swiss FDPIC.

Data protection officer: we have not appointed one, because our processing does not meet the conditions that require it (Art. 37 GDPR). Contact us at the address above. EU/UK representative: none has been appointed. Requests from the EU, the UK and Türkiye (KVKK) are handled at erengn@gmail.com.

For users in Türkiye the Turkish privacy policy and KVKK notice applies. The notice under Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act is in the Traditional Chinese version. Users in Hong Kong and Macau can exercise their rights under local law by emailing us.

14. Children

Catbeam is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country).

15. Changes

If this policy changes, the current version is published on this page with its date. If the kinds of data that leave your iPhone change, we will also tell you in the app before any new upload.
