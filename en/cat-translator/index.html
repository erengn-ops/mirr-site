Cat translator

A playful guess at what your cat's meow is about, made on your iPhone.

Updated: 2026-11-24

Catbeam's cat translator is for fun. It checks that it really hears a cat, then turns the pitch, length and loudness of the meow into one of seven playful moods. It does not measure what your cat actually means, and no app can turn a meow into words.

Get Catbeam on the App Store
iPhone, iOS 18 or later. Free to download.

How it works

  1. Open Listen and hold your iPhone near your cat while it meows, or load a recording.
  2. Apple's built-in sound recognition, running on your iPhone, checks that a cat is audible (meow, purr or other cat sounds). Speech or silence is not translated: no cat, no translation.
  3. The app measures the pitch, length and loudness of the sound and shows one of seven moods: greeting, wants attention, might be hungry, relaxed and happy, curious, complaining or uneasy.

What it can't do

Cats meow mostly at people, and each cat's meows differ. The moods above are a rule of thumb for fun, not a diagnosis and not a scientific reading. If your cat suddenly meows much more, hides or stops eating, ask a vet.

Privacy

The sound is analysed only on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. Catbeam needs no account.

What else is in Catbeam

  • A free health journal: vaccine card with days until the next dose, medication reminders, weight chart, vet visits and parasite treatments.
  • A pet passport with the microchip number (not an official document) and games for cats.
  • Optional: bring your own cat to life from a few photos. It is made with AI, so it may not look exactly like your cat; it is a one-time purchase. Catbeam Club is an optional yearly subscription.

Questions

Is a cat translator real?

No app can translate a meow into words. Catbeam's translator is for fun: it confirms a cat is audible and maps the sound's pitch, length and loudness to a playful mood.

Is the cat translator free?

Yes. Downloading Catbeam is free, and the translator, the health journal and the games are free.

Is my cat's sound uploaded?

No. The sound is analysed only on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.

Is there an Android version?

No. Catbeam is made for iPhone and needs iOS 18 or later.
