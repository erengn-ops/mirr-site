Support

Email us: erengn@gmail.com
In the app: Settings → Support → "Contact us". We usually reply within 2 working days.

Frequently asked questions

Is there a subscription?
No. Catbeam is free; "Catbeam Plus" and bringing your own cat to life ("Bring Your Cat to Life") are one-time purchases, and a cat's special-day clip is a separate one-time purchase too; "Special Days, All Year" is a one-time purchase valid for 12 months that does not renew. Nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.

I changed phones and "Bring Your Cat to Life" is missing. How do I restore purchases?
Open the "Bring Your Cat to Life" or Catbeam Plus purchase screen and tap "Restore Purchases"; you need to be signed in with the same Apple Account. "Extra Cat", "My Cat Grew Up", "Special Day Clip" and "Special Days, All Year" are consumables and cannot be restored. For your cats and records to reach the new phone, see the iCloud question below.

How do my cat and records reach my new phone? (iCloud)
Sign in with the same Apple Account and make sure Catbeam is switched on in iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud. Your cat profiles, health records and memories come from your own iCloud; your cat's videos and special-day clips are large files, so they download over Wi-Fi first (not automatically over mobile data). When a pack has fully downloaded, your cat settles in. If iCloud is off or full nothing is lost: new records stay on your phone and sync when iCloud is on or space is freed. Your iPhone backup carries the data too. Our server does not see anything that syncs; details: Privacy Policy.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. No new cats are created for a refunded purchase; cats already on your iPhone stay.

What is Catbeam Plus?
A one-time purchase that adds unlimited cats, travel passport and full health report PDFs, all games, the full weight and translator history and more. Vaccines, medication, health reminders and the vaccine card stay free. Plus can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. "Bring Your Cat to Life" already includes Plus.

Why does the care guide allow 5 questions a day?
Free use includes 5 smart care guide questions a day; with Catbeam Plus or "Bring Your Cat to Life" you get 30 a day. Emergency questions are always answered.

What is "Extra Cat"?
A one-time purchase for bringing another of your cats to life. It includes one creation and, if needed, up to two retries, and is used up once the cat is created.

What is "My Cat Grew Up"?
A one-time purchase that creates your living cat again from new photos, for example when your kitten has grown. It is used up once the new cat is created.

What is a "Special Day Clip"?
A one-time purchase that prepares a short festive video of your cat in its own room for its birthday, home day or a holiday (one day, one cat). On the day your iPhone shows a local notification and offers the clip; nothing is sent to our server until you buy it. Making the clip takes about 10-20 minutes.

How does "Special Days, All Year" work?
For one cat it prepares a clip for each of seven special days over 12 months (birthday, home day, Christmas, New Year, Lunar New Year, Halloween, Cat Day); one clip per day. For the birthday and home-day clips you need to have entered those dates for the cat. Your iPhone tries early on the day in the background; if that does not happen, opening the app on that day or within the next 7 days is enough. Our server does not keep your cat's dates or pictures for this pack, and "Delete all my data" also deletes the pack's server record. It does not renew; it ends after 12 months.

How long does it take to bring my cat to life?
After you start, your cat usually arrives in about 10 minutes (it can take a little longer when we are busy); the app shows progress and an estimate. You can close the app: if you allowed notifications you get a "has arrived" notification when your cat is ready. After this first appearance, more of your cat's moves can keep arriving for a while.

It said "Not this time" or got stuck while making my cat. Do I lose my right?
No, your right is not lost. If the problem is on our side (provider outage, server restart, unexpected error) your purchase is not counted as used; the job often resumes by itself, and if not you retry for free with the retry button in the app. Only cases that come from your photos (for example photos where the cat cannot be seen or that are very blurry, or the safety filter) count against the 2 retries per purchase; in that case try again with sharp, bright photos of a single cat. If it still does not work after a few tries, contact us from Settings → Contact us and we will finish your cat with you. If you want your money back, ask Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com); for a refunded purchase our server does not create a new cat or clip, and cats already on your iPhone stay.

My cat did not come to life, or does not look like my cat.
Bright photos of a single cat, with the whole body and face visible, work best. If the problem continues, email us with the approximate time you started.

What happens to my photos?
They are used only to create your living cat. Our server holds them in memory during the job and does not write them to disk; the job record is deleted at the latest 7 days after your iPhone has received your cat, and never later than 30 days after the cat is ready; photo analysis and generation are done by Anthropic and Higgsfield (and Higgsfield's partner models) in the USA. Details: Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Data → "Delete all my data". Your records are kept on your iPhone and, if iCloud sync is on, in your own iCloud; this deletes both, and also the server record of a "Special Days, All Year" entitlement. Other records on our server are deleted automatically within the periods above; email us if you want them gone sooner.

Is the translation real?
No, the cat translator is for fun: it is a playful guess based on the pitch, length and loudness of the sound, and does not measure what your cat is really saying. The sound is analysed only on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere. The smart care guide gives general information only and does not replace a vet.
