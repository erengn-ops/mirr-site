Frequently asked questions

Is there a subscription?
No. Meowly is free; "Meowly Plus" and bringing your own cat to life ("Bring Your Cat to Life") are one-time purchases. Nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.

I changed phones and "Bring Your Cat to Life" is missing.
Tap "Restore Purchases" on the purchase screen. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account. "Extra Cat" and "My Cat Grew Up" are consumables and cannot be restored, but cats you already made stay in your old phone's backup.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com. No new cats are created for a refunded purchase; cats already on your iPhone stay.

What is Meowly Plus?
A one-time purchase that adds unlimited cats, travel passport and full health report PDFs, all games, the full weight and translator history and more. Vaccines, medication, health reminders and the vaccine card stay free. Plus can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. "Bring Your Cat to Life" already includes Plus.

Why does the assistant allow 5 questions a day?
Free use includes 5 care assistant questions a day; with Meowly Plus or "Bring Your Cat to Life" you get 30 a day. Emergency questions are always answered.

What is "Extra Cat"?
A one-time purchase for bringing another of your cats to life. It includes one creation and, if needed, up to two retries, and is used up once the cat is created.

What is "My Cat Grew Up"?
A one-time purchase that creates your living cat again from new photos, for example when your kitten has grown. It is used up once the new cat is created.

My cat did not come to life, or does not look like my cat.
Bright photos of a single cat, with the whole body and face visible, work best. If the problem continues, email us with the approximate time you started.

What happens to my photos?
They are used only to create your living cat. Our server holds them in memory during the job and deletes the job record within 24 hours; photo analysis and generation are done by Anthropic and Higgsfield in the USA. Details: Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?
Settings → Data → "Delete all my data". Your records are stored only on your iPhone.

Is the translation real?
No, it is a playful guess. The health assistant gives general information only and does not replace a vet.

Why is the app called Miyav in some places?
Miyav is the app's Turkish name; it is the same app.
