Tap

Give them a poke. They answer.

Every reaction is a real clip made from your cat's photos: love, a meow, a happy wiggle. A different one with each tap.

Day and night

Sleeps at night, wakes when you do.

At night your cat curls up and sleeps, flicking an ear now and then. The moment you open the app, they lift their head.

All in one place

Everything a cat needs.

Special days

Birthdays, New Year, adoption anniversaries… a short celebration clip for your cat, as a separate one-time add-on.

Smart care guide

General information for the health and behavior questions that come up. It doesn't diagnose and doesn't replace your vet.

Everywhere, with iCloud

Your cat, records and clips sync through iCloud between iPhones on the same Apple account. A new phone finds them waiting.

Health journal

Vaccines, medication, weight and reminders in one place. You can export your records any time, free.

Cat translator

Turns meows into a playful translation. Sound is analyzed on your iPhone; the result is a fun guess.

Price

Catbeam is free to download. Bringing your own cat to life is a one-time purchase: no subscription, nothing that renews by itself. You'll see the current price on the App Store.
