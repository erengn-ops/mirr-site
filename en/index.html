Your cat, alive on your iPhone.

Catbeam is a cat app for iPhone. It turns a few photos of your cat into an animated cat that lives on your phone, translates meows for fun, keeps the vaccine card and medication schedule, and has games for cats.

  • Your own living cat: an animated likeness generated with AI from 1–4 photos.
  • Cat translator: sound is analysed on your iPhone; the result is a playful guess.
  • Health journal: vaccinations, medication, weight and reminders. Stored on your iPhone.
  • Health and behaviour assistant: general information only; it does not diagnose and is not a vet.
  • Games and memories.

No tracking, no account.
