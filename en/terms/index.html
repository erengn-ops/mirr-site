1. Free features and one-time purchases

  • Catbeam is free to download and use. For one cat, everything needed for its health is free: the cat translator, cat passport, vaccine and parasite tracking with every health reminder, medications, vet visits, the vaccine card PDF, weight log (3-month chart), journal, three games, three daily routine reminders, data export and the smart care guide (5 questions a day; emergency questions are always answered).
  • "Catbeam Plus" is a non-consumable in-app purchase with no time limit. It adds unlimited cats, the travel passport PDF, the full health report PDF, the full weight history, unlimited routine reminders, all games and game timers, the memory book PDF, the full translator history and 30 smart care guide questions a day. It can be restored on your other devices and is available through Family Sharing. Nothing you created is ever deleted: if Plus ends (for example after a refund), your existing cats, reminders and records are kept, and you can always export all of your records for free.
  • "Bring Your Cat to Life" is a non-consumable in-app purchase. It unlocks the living version of your own cat (a set of short video clips in which your cat, in its own room, meows, stretches, plays, naps and reacts to you) for one cat, with no time limit. It includes creating that cat, with up to two retries if a result fails, and includes everything in Catbeam Plus. You can restore it on other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. It is not available through Family Sharing.
  • "Extra Cat" is a consumable in-app purchase that creates the living version of another cat: one creation, with up to two retries if a result fails. It is used up once the cat is created and cannot be restored.
  • "My Cat Grew Up" is a consumable in-app purchase that creates your living cat once more from new photos, for example when your kitten has grown, with up to two retries if a result fails. It is used up once the new cat is created and cannot be restored.
  • "Special Day Clip" is a consumable in-app purchase. It prepares one short video clip for one special day of one cat (birthday, home day or a holiday). The price is shown on the purchase screen before you buy. If the clip cannot be made, your purchase is kept and the clip is tried again. Once the clip is made the purchase is used up and cannot be restored. The clip is made from the AI-generated picture of your cat in its own room and may contain festive elements such as a hat or a cake. It is not included in "Bring Your Cat to Life" or "Extra Cat"; it is bought separately.
  • "Special Days, All Year" is no longer sold from Catbeam 1.2 (the special-day clips are part of Catbeam Club). If you already bought it, it keeps working until its 12 months end, as described here. For 12 months from the moment you bought it, it entitles you to one clip for each of that cat's seven special days (birthday, home day, Christmas, New Year, Lunar New Year, Halloween and Cat Day); one clip per day at most. The birthday and home-day clips depend on your having entered those dates for the cat in the app. To make a clip the app must be able to run (be opened) on that day or within the following 7 days; a day that is not started in that window stays unused and ends when the 12 months end. We do not refund unused days (refunds are handled by Apple only, see section 3). If a clip cannot be made, that day is not counted as used and can be tried again. The pack cannot be moved to another cat; each cat needs its own purchase. It does not renew when the term ends. Because it is a consumable it cannot be restored with "Restore Purchases"; if iCloud sync is on, the entitlement details reach your new iPhone together with your cat. The price is shown on the purchase screen before you buy.
  • "Catbeam Club" is an optional auto-renewing yearly subscription (see section 2).
  • Apart from "Catbeam Club" (below), none of these purchases is a subscription: nothing renews automatically (including the 12-month "Special Days, All Year" pack, if you already own one) and there is no free trial. Payment is charged once to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Prices are shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. All purchases are processed by Apple.

2. Catbeam Club (auto-renewing subscription)

  • What it is: "Catbeam Club" is a yearly auto-renewing subscription (product "mirr.club.year", one subscription group, one plan; there is no weekly or monthly plan). It adds, for the cat you choose as your main cat, a new short clip every month, a clip for each of your cat's seven special days (birthday, home day, Christmas, New Year, Lunar New Year, Halloween and Cat Day) per membership year, a few more daily moments a day ("One more moment") and sharing without the Catbeam brand mark. Everything free stays free; nothing you already have is taken away when Club ends.
  • Price and free trial: the price for your country and the length of the term are shown on the subscription screen before you subscribe (for example, 7 days free, then the yearly price). New subscribers get a 7-day free trial, once per Apple Account for this subscription group; if you are not eligible, the screen does not offer a trial. The trial is for evaluating the subscription: during the trial the app does not make new Club clips; the first new clip can be made once the first paid year has started.
  • Payment and renewal: payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the subscription (after the free trial, if you have one). The subscription renews automatically for another year at the then-current price unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account is charged for the renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • Managing and cancelling: in your iPhone's Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Catbeam Club (or from the app's Club screen, "Manage subscription"). Deleting the app does not cancel it. If you cancel, Club stays active until the end of the period you paid for; during a free trial you can cancel before it ends to avoid being charged. In the app you can turn on a reminder before the trial ends.
  • Refunds: payments are processed by Apple and we cannot refund them ourselves. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com; if you live in the EU or the UK, Apple's terms give you a 14-day right of withdrawal for App Store purchases. If Apple refunds or ends your subscription, our server makes no new Club clips for it; cats, clips and records already on your iPhone stay. A clip that is already being made when a refund happens may still be delivered.
  • Price changes: if the price changes, Apple tells you and, where the law or Apple's rules require it, asks for your consent before the new price applies.
  • Fair use: Club clips are made on demand with AI under a yearly fair-use limit per membership (and a short-term limit on how quickly clips can be started). When you reach it, the app says "That is all for today, more tomorrow" or that this month's clip is made; the next monthly clip comes the next month. One main cat per membership (changeable once a month); a membership is tied to the Apple Account that bought it and is not shared through Family Sharing.
  • If a clip fails: if a Club clip cannot be made because of a problem on our side or a provider outage, that month's or day's slot is not counted as used and can be tried again. We do not guarantee any particular result (such as an exact likeness).
  • Availability: Club needs your own cat's room (made with "Bring Your Cat to Life") and is not sold in every country. If the Club server is unreachable, the parts of Club that run on your iPhone keep working.
  • Restore: use "Restore Purchases" on the subscription screen to restore Club on another device signed in with the same Apple Account.

3. Failed creation, refunds and restoring purchases

  • If creation fails (your right is not lost): if your cat or clip cannot be made because of a problem on our side, a provider outage or a server restart, your purchase is not counted as used: the job continues from where it stopped by itself where possible, otherwise you can restart it for free with the retry button in the app. Only cases where the problem comes from your photos count against the retries (for example results blocked by the safety filter or that do not look enough like your cat); for "Bring Your Cat to Life", "Extra Cat" and "My Cat Grew Up" that means one creation and two retries per purchase. If it still does not work, email us and we will finish your cat with you. We do not guarantee any particular result (such as an exact likeness). A refund of the amount paid is made only by Apple (below).
  • All payments are processed by Apple; we cannot issue refunds ourselves. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you live in the EU or the UK, Apple's terms give you a 14-day right of withdrawal for App Store purchases. If a purchase is refunded, our server does not create new cats or clips for that purchase; cats already on your iPhone stay.
  • Use "Restore Purchases" on the purchase screen to restore "Bring Your Cat to Life" and "Catbeam Plus" on another device signed in with the same Apple Account. "Extra Cat", "My Cat Grew Up", "Special Day Clip" and "Special Days, All Year" are consumables and cannot be restored. Catbeam Club is an auto-renewing subscription and can be restored with Restore Purchases (see the Club section).

4. AI-generated content

  • Your cat's animations are generated by AI from the photos you send (for special-day clips, the yearly pack and Club clips, from your cat's existing picture in its room). Results may not look exactly like your cat and may contain errors or unexpected imagery. They do not show your cat's real behaviour. A Club clip is an AI-generated short video that shows an imagined moment of your cat in its room; it does not show your cat's real behaviour.
  • Only send photos you own or have the right to use. Photos must not contain people, other people's content or objectionable material.
  • You may use the generated animations for personal, non-commercial purposes.

5. Health information is not veterinary advice

  • The smart care guide, articles and reminders provide general information. They do not diagnose, do not recommend treatment or doses, and do not replace a veterinary examination.
  • Vaccination and medication entries are what you enter; enter medicines and doses as your vet prescribed.
  • In an emergency (difficulty breathing, seizures, inability to urinate, suspected poisoning and similar) contact a vet immediately.

6. Cat translator

The translation is a heuristic mapping of pitch, length and loudness. It is a playful guess and does not scientifically measure what your cat means. The sound is analysed on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere.
