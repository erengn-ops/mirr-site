Terms of Use (EULA)

Last updated: 6 October 2026 · The English version prevails over translations, except in Türkiye where the Turkish version applies.

Catbeam is licensed to you under Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement (Standard EULA). The terms below supplement it; if they conflict, the Standard EULA prevails.

1. Free features and one-time purchases

  • Catbeam is free to download and use. For one cat, everything needed for its health is free: the cat translator, cat passport, vaccine and parasite tracking with every health reminder, medications, vet visits, the vaccine card PDF, weight log (3-month chart), journal, three games, three daily routine reminders, data export and the care assistant (5 questions a day; emergency questions are always answered).
  • "Catbeam Plus" is a non-consumable in-app purchase with no time limit. It adds unlimited cats, the travel passport PDF, the full health report PDF, the full weight history, unlimited routine reminders, all games and game timers, the memory book PDF, the full translator history and 30 care assistant questions a day. It can be restored on your other devices and is available through Family Sharing. Nothing you created is ever deleted: if Plus ends (for example after a refund), your existing cats, reminders and records are kept, and you can always export all of your records for free.
  • "Bring Your Cat to Life" is a non-consumable in-app purchase. It unlocks the living version of your own cat (a set of short video clips in which your cat meows, stretches, plays, naps and reacts to you) for one cat, with no time limit. It includes creating that cat, with up to two retries if a result fails, and includes everything in Catbeam Plus. You can restore it on other devices signed in with the same Apple Account. It is not available through Family Sharing.
  • "Extra Cat" is a consumable in-app purchase that creates the living version of another cat: one creation, with up to two retries if a result fails. It is used up once the cat is created and cannot be restored.
  • "My Cat Grew Up" is a consumable in-app purchase that creates your living cat once more from new photos, for example when your kitten has grown, with up to two retries if a result fails. It is used up once the new cat is created and cannot be restored.
  • None of these purchases is a subscription: nothing renews automatically and there is no free trial. Payment is charged once to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase. Prices are shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. All purchases are processed by Apple.

2. Refunds and restoring purchases

  • All payments are processed by Apple; we cannot issue refunds ourselves. Request a refund from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If you live in the EU or the UK, Apple's terms give you a 14-day right of withdrawal for App Store purchases. If a purchase is refunded, our server does not create new cats for that purchase; cats already on your iPhone stay.
  • Use "Restore Purchases" on the purchase screen to restore "Bring Your Cat to Life" and "Catbeam Plus" on another device signed in with the same Apple Account. "Extra Cat" and "My Cat Grew Up" are consumables and cannot be restored.

3. AI-generated content

  • Your cat's animations are generated by AI from the photos you send. Results may not look exactly like your cat and may contain errors or unexpected imagery. They do not show your cat's real behaviour.
  • Only send photos you own or have the right to use. Photos must not contain people, other people's content or objectionable material.
  • You may use the generated animations for personal, non-commercial purposes.

4. Health information is not veterinary advice

  • The health and behaviour assistant, articles and reminders provide general information. They do not diagnose, do not recommend treatment or doses, and do not replace a veterinary examination.
  • Vaccination and medication entries are what you enter; enter medicines and doses as your vet prescribed.
  • In an emergency (difficulty breathing, seizures, inability to urinate, suspected poisoning and similar) contact a vet immediately.

5. Cat translator

The translation is a heuristic mapping of pitch, length and loudness. It is a playful guess and does not scientifically measure what your cat means.
